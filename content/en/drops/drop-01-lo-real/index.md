Conceived as a direct reaction against sterile digital perfection. Drop 01 collects 12 garments crafted from custom-milled loopback fleeces and heavyweight cottons exceeding 400 GSM. Each garment was hand-printed in the historic printing workshops of Barracas and La Boca. Zero mass production: when the roll run terminates, the drop archives permanently.
