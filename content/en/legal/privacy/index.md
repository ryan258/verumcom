This site is static. It sets no first-party or third-party cookies and uses no advertising trackers, analytics or commercial profiling. Fonts, images and files are served from the site itself. The hosting service may log technical connection data, such as the IP address, under its own policies.

There are no active forms for now. If you email us, we use your message only to reply. If we later switch on the contact form or the newsletter, an external provider will process that data (Formspree for contact, Buttondown for the newsletter) and we will say so here before switching them on.

We do not sell your data or use it for anything other than replying to you or sending what you asked for.
