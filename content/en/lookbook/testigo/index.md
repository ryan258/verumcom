Testigo is the collection in miniature. A red cap, curved brim and three embroidered marks recalling a print registration. Cobalt, yellow and ecru separate the layers against the red base.

A conventional metal buckle adjusts the fit without adding another symbol. The cap accompanies the tee or hoodie but works on its own: a small everyday signal.
