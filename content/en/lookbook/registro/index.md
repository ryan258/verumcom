The first layer of Sobreimpreso. Registro places three deliberately offset blocks across the chest: red, cobalt and acid yellow. Their displacement belongs to the artwork, not to a manufacturing error.

The back stays almost empty, with a VERUM × LF signature below the collar. An everyday tee that lets the gesture read without turning the entire body into a poster.
