Trazo brings forward the marks usually erased when a job is finished. Against black, an open frame and its construction lines read like chalk; the proposed print is permanent, not a mark designed to rub off.

The hoodie remains straightforward: double-layer hood, kangaroo pocket and ribbed cuffs. Experimentation happens in the drawing, not in uncomfortable construction.
