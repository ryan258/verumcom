Escuadra reduces the workshop vocabulary to an angle waiting for another line. White embroidery sits on the front of a graphite cap, with the collaboration signature at the side.

The curved brim and adjustable buckle are conventional. No ornamental metal or custom fittings: the tool becomes a sign, not extra weight.
