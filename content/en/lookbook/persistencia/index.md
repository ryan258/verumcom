Persistencia changes the scale of the gesture. Cobalt fills the garment; red and yellow appear on the back, like layers of one poster shifted by time. QUEDA—what remains—is the only large word.

The front keeps a small embroidered signature and a conventional kangaroo pocket. No tears or artificial aging: memory lives in the composition, and actual wear can arrive through use.
