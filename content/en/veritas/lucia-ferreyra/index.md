[[chapters]]
title = "01 / Looking again"
piece = "registro"
questions = [
  ["What makes you keep a scrap of paper?",
   "Evidence that it was used. I am not looking for the prettiest sign; I am interested in something that served a purpose and then became displaced. A letter cut off by another poster, an arrow that no longer points anywhere. Taking it away does not mean I can take a whole neighborhood with me. I take a question: which part of this would still mean something on another surface?"],
  ["How do you keep memory from becoming decorative nostalgia?",
   "I am not rebuilding a perfect era. I work with what is missing and what somebody covered. That is why I did not draw a complete old sign for Registro: three fields and an offset remain. The ecru space between inks matters as much as the color. That is where the wearer comes in; they do not need to know my story to find their own."],
]

[[chapters]]
title = "02 / Another layer"
piece = "persistencia"
questions = [
  ["Why red, cobalt and acid yellow?",
   "I wanted colors that could argue without needing twenty other elements. Red moves forward, cobalt holds things together and yellow interrupts. On paper I can let one ink mix with another. On a garment I need to decide where each shape ends so it reads in motion. I am not copying a workshop effect; I am translating the tension I like about it."],
  ["What changes when a poster becomes the back of a hoodie?",
   "The distance changes. You examine a sheet up close; you see a back as someone walks away. Persistencia has to work that way. So I enlarged the layers and kept one word: QUEDA—what remains. It does not mean nothing changes. It means that even after change, we carry something with us. The front is quieter: a signature and the blue. I did not want the garment shouting from every side."],
]

[[chapters]]
title = "03 / As little as it takes"
piece = "testigo"
questions = [
  ["What do you keep when you reduce everything to a cap?",
   "The movement between layers. Testigo no longer has a poster: it has three bars starting at different heights. Embroidery needs less detail, which forces a choice. If I need to tell the whole story for the mark to work, I have not solved it yet. I like someone seeing three colored marks first, then finding the conversation later if they want to."],
  ["What would need to happen before this collection became real?",
   "Testing and listening. Seeing ink on cotton, checking which details embroidery can hold and adjusting placement on a worn garment. Also tracing any fragment I wanted to use: other people’s memories are not free material just because they are on the street. This proposal uses invented forms. A real collaboration would need to speak with its sources, credit them and agree on what can be transformed."],
]
