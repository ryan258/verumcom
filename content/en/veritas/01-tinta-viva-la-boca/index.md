Nico's studio sits at the deep end of a historic tenement off Pedro de Mendoza. To reach the entrance requires dodging corrugated tin sheets and 20-liter industrial latex pails.

> "Design schools train you to measure in fractions of millimeters and calibrate Pantone profiles. The street teaches you that rust eats ink alive, and if your guard drops, the river wind tears down your scaffolding."

Fasolo paints under cover of dark, when heavy dock traffic ebbs and harbor searchlights bounce off the Riachuelo basin. His craft bypasses digital projection: freehand sketches in tailor's chalk followed by heavy floor-roller fills. The dialogue with VERUM originated right on this floor over bitter mate, witnessing how thick screenprinting ink scarred grey fleece without apology.

That tactile, permanent scar on heavy cotton is the exact standard driving the Verdad Bomber piece.
