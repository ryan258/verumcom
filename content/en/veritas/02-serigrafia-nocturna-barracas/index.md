At 3:00 AM the depot on Calle Patricios vibrates with sub-bass instrumentals and the blunt clack of squeegees against 43-count screen mesh.

> "If a shirt carries a bleeding droplet or a two-millimeter registration shift, it does not hit the bin. That imperfection is physical proof of human hands over an automated assembly line."

Matías and Clara founded Taller 420 seven years back. They reject corporate promo runs and polyester blanks. Their tables handle half-kilo fleece, water-based biological discharge pigments, and natural 48-hour cure cycles hung across structural pipe racks.

Every single VERUM piece passes through their workspace. This is why every garment label carries its batch sequence penned by hand in indelible ink.
