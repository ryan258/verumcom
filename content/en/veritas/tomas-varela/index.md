[[chapters]]
title = "01 / Measuring a possibility"
piece = "cota"
questions = [
  ["What interests you about a workshop mark?",
   "It is still thinking. A dimension suggests a size; a chalk line proposes a cut. Then the material answers. If you erase every mark, the object seems to have always known what it would become. I want to show decisions, doubts and corrections. That does not make something less solid. It gives the object its history back."],
  ["Why leave the shape on Cota open?",
   "An open corner changes how you look. You stop seeing a finished rectangle and start completing something. On the tee, 01 and 02 refer to stages of an idea, not actual measurements or instructions. I wanted the voice of a drawing without turning the body into a manual. White acts as the sheet; black needs to stay simple enough to read from a distance."],
]

[[chapters]]
title = "02 / What stays visible"
piece = "trazo"
questions = [
  ["Your collection is about change, but it uses no color. Why?",
   "So the change happens in relationships. A line can approach another, stop short or leave room. I do not need a separate color for every decision. Black, white and graphite let me separate ground, mark and pause. I also like garments that can enter an existing life without asking somebody to invent a whole character to wear them."],
  ["What did you bring from metalwork into the Trazo hoodie?",
   "The drawing before the object. I did not want to hang hardware from it or simulate armor. The hoodie has familiar construction; its back holds an open frame and auxiliary lines like those you use to check a direction. White ink recalls chalk, but the proposal is for a permanent print. I do not want a garment that loses its idea on first wear. Appearance has to speak to how something works."],
]

[[chapters]]
title = "03 / Leaving room"
piece = "escuadra"
questions = [
  ["How does all that become a cap?",
   "By taking things away. Escuadra has an angle and a line that does not touch it. Your eye makes the joint. The embroidery does not need to look like a tiny tool; it needs to preserve that distance. Graphite softens the contrast compared with the hoodie, but the white thread stays clear. The buckle adjusts the cap and that is all. Not every piece of metal has to carry a message."],
  ["How would you know the collection was finished?",
   "When its decisions had been tested, not when every question disappeared. We would need to check printed line widths, embroidery durability and fit on different bodies. This is a proposal, not an approved production specification. Leaving a joint open in the drawing does not excuse an unresolved seam. For me, admitting that something can change demands more care with what you actually hand over."],
]
