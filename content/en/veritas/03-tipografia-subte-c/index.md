Koba salvages lead and wood types from shuttered letterpress operations in San Telmo. Utilizing a 1940 proofing press, she strikes poster batches later wheatpasted onto Retiro transit columns.

> "Street typography must shout within two seconds. Commuters rushing for the Tigre train don't pause for nuance: they register blunt contrast, weight, and urgency."

That dense, condensed typographic pressure—heavy black strokes with zero air between stems—governs VERUM's visual architecture. Friction designed to seize the gaze.
