Operating from a basement steeped in coal smoke and machine oil, Cacho hand-forges the solid steel zipper pulls and rivets anchoring our outerwear.

> "Disposable retail slaps fragile plastic zippers that shear off on month two. Legitimate clothing must survive a decade of packed buses, rain, and street wear."

Every metal component on a VERUM garment is heat-tempered and darkened with gunsmith bluing, avoiding cheap flashiness. It carries the muted patina of vintage ironwork in southern Buenos Aires.
